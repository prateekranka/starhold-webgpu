# Starhold — world and rendering plan

Binding design companion to `DIRECTIVE.md` and `INTERFACE.md`. Assumption: this is an autonomous, endless spectacle with inspectable inhabitants; no orders, building placement, resource spending, panning, or victory screen. All dimensions below are world tiles unless labeled pixels. Initial seed: **73129**.

## Setting and factions

**The Vesper March:** a chartered star civilization raises a luminous fortress-town on an amethyst frontier, forging fallen-star ore into architecture while raiders descend through a broken alien causeway. Medieval town-building rhythms meet armored science fantasy: buttresses, pennants, crystal reactors, cargo caravans, siege beasts. Technology looks assembled and inhabited, never like floating abstract neon boxes.

| Faction | Name and color ownership | Silhouette language |
|---|---|---|
| Player | **Dawnward Compact**; teal armor, ivory edges, gold heraldry, cyan energy | Broad square foundations, stepped ivory shoulders, split cathedral fins; human-scale doors, ladders, flags and exposed construction ribs. Workers are small and round; soldiers upright; carriers wide. |
| Hostile | **Cinderwake Reavers**; wine armor, vermilion edges, orange weapons | Low asymmetric wedges, backward hooks, serrated silhouettes; red cloth tails. Fast infantry contrasts with long, jointed siege walkers. Never recolored player shapes. |

## Terrain and layout

- Fixed 32×32 island; coordinates `x,y ∈ [0,32]`; north is decreasing y. Default yaw projects +x down-right and +y down-left. Terrain is static and identical for a seed; decorative jitter ≤0.2 tile, kept off paths.
- Basalt mesa at height z=0.5 contains x=7..24,y=9..25, with an eastern defense terrace x=23..28,y=7..16 and southeast expansion shelf x=23..28,y=20..25. Ore crescent x=2..8,y=19..28 at z=0 surrounds a western industrial shelf x=3..9,y=15..25 at z=0.5; exclude foundations/roads from ore placement. Violet crystal garden x=7..13,y=3..8 at z=1; raider causeway x=24..31,y=2..14 at z=0.5. Outer rim drops to z=-1, with isolated hexagonal basalt columns and visible cliff faces.
- Roads: depot→ore through (5,25); depot→HQ through (11,20),(13,18); depot→forge through (8,20),(8,17); HQ→barracks through (18.5,14); HQ→front through (21,14),(24,14). Routes connect doorway aprons, never building centers. Width 1.5 tiles, pale worn flagstones, broken edges. Two ramp lanes, each 2 tiles wide, bridge all road height changes; no units climb vertical cliffs. Reserve roads before placing buildings.
- Foreground x=19..27,y=23..29: broken arch foot, sparse ochre grass, two low crystal clumps, dust channel. Keep everything here below 10 screen pixels tall. Background edge: thin lilac haze bands, three moons/rocks too small to read as UI; no large horizon or sky taking space from the map.
- Tile surfaces use 2–3 colors, hand-sized clusters of 2–5 pixels, sparse diagonal cracks; never independent random pixel noise. Cliff sides have vertical striations and alternating ledges. Buildings use snapped voxel-like geometry with beveled corners and shaded faces, rendered anew at all four yaws.
- Ground diamonds are nominally 12×7 pixels at default zoom (continuous geometry 12×6.928 before pixel snapping), matching the binding 35.264° pitch. z=1 raises a point 6.928 pixels. Smallest walkable height increment 0.5 tile. Do not substitute a 30° camera to force exact 2:1 tiles.
- Fixed world light from northwest/above. Top surfaces brightest, southeast faces darkest; ambient/contact shadows use palette colors with ordered coverage, not translucent black. Correct orthographic depth test for geometry; depth-tested feet rings/particles. Roofs can hide units; a selected hidden unit gets a thin dashed ground ring and 3-pixel chevron above the occluder, without roof transparency.

Individual pieces rise from deliveries or crane hooks, never scale the entire building from zero. Unbuilt volume remains empty. Percentage is completed labor, not wall-clock time.

## Economy, scheduling and endless growth

- Two visible stocks: **alloy** (gold ingots) and **charge** (cyan cells). Miners chip violet star-ore nodes for 2 s per chunk, hold up to four chunks, walk to Freight Court, and drop them for 0.8 s. Starting Court's compact press converts four chunks→four alloy in 2 s; Starforge upgrades the shared process to four→six in 2 s, with no teleporting ore: Beetle carries Court ore crates to forge. Beetles hold 16 chunks or one site's reserved kit; skiffs hold one export/relief crate. Store max 300 alloy; surplus keeps circulating as export crates at Wharf.
- Heliowell produces 2 charge/s, stored to 200. Initial treasury: 160 alloy, 120 charge; Court begins with 16 ore chunks. Buildings reserve their entire cost when ordered; two builders and one Beetle are dedicated to the active site. Preconstruction delivery takes at least 6 s, extended to actual route travel plus 0.8 s at each endpoint; construction clock begins only after arrival. Durations below assume unobstructed planned routes.
- Costs A/C: Keep 100/60, Court 24/0, Well 32/20, Hall 28/12, Forge 36/16, Hearth 20/8, Bastion 40/24, Wharf 48/24. Active labor costs no further resources. Repair costs 1 alloy per 25 restored HP at 25 HP/s per Riveter. Heliowell and Court provide a bootstrap path even after loss of the Forge.
- Preloaded opening queue has materials already at its first four sites; two builder pairs relay between sites, with the resting pair drawn from repair staff and walking to the next apron before its start tick. Initial complete buildings: Keep (16,16), Court (7,23), Well (10,12), Bastion (24,8). Hall (16,11) builds t=0–24; Forge (5,18) t=24–54; Hearth (17,21) t=54–78; second Bastion (26,12) t=78–120. Their costs are already deducted from the stated initial treasury. Half-tile centers are allowed for even/odd footprint alignment. These are normal simulated jobs with pre-reserved materials; later damage can delay them, but protected opening routes keep the canonical frame on schedule.
- Wharf (21.5,22) starts no earlier than t=126, after cost reservation/delivery, and takes 30 s. Thereafter populate exactly three additional pads: Hearth (13,24), Well (7,13), Court (26,22), earliest t=180/240/300 respectively, with normal delivery windows and costs. These complete the **12-building cap**; no road/pad overlap. Keep 1-tile clear gap between neighboring footprint edges except road-connected aprons.
- At cap, every 24 s choose next completed non-anchor building by stable ID and assemble an external machinery module for 18 s (8 alloy/4 charge); keep building functional. Six seconds survey/scaffold, six seconds seat new panels, six seconds commission. Old module is removed by crane into a salvage crate when the cycle begins; it refunds four alloy. Before cap, start the same refurbishment after 6 s without major construction, using the idle builder pair; finish it before that pair accepts another site. This is visible refurbishment, never instant teardown or invisible exponential growth.
- Unit replacement priority: workers/carriers→Sentinels→Sunlances→Skiffs. Riveter 4A/0C, Beetle 8/2, Sentinel 8/4, Sunlance 10/6, Skiff 12/6; one replacement every 6 s from Keep/Hall/Wharf as appropriate. Reserve 24 alloy for replacements/repairs after opening queue. If treasury stays below 12 alloy for 15 s, Keep receives a clearly visible relief capsule containing 24 alloy; no hidden balance injection.
- Ore: 12 nodes, each 80 chunks. At depletion the shard retracts; after 20 s cyan cracks appear, over 4 s a new violet shard rises, restoring 80. Only reserve nodes on opposite side of active miners regenerate simultaneously; workers reroute to nearest available node. No finite resource exhaustion.
- Pathing is deterministic 8-neighbor tile navigation, no diagonal corner cutting. Buildings and cliffs block; roads are cheaper. Friendly units queue, yielding after 0.8 s and choosing the next free adjacent lane by stable ID. Hostiles never enter the economic courtyard while defenders exist; they engage the eastern defense screen.

## Combat and waves

- First raid t=36 s; subsequent raids every 30 s forever. Composition cycles every four waves: 4 Jackals; 6 Jackals; 6 Jackals + 1 Strider; 8 Jackals + 2 Striders. Spawn off the right causeway near (31,2), stagger infantry by 1.2 s; Striders arrive at wave+8 s and wave+9.5 s. Follow (29,5)→(27,8)→(27,10). At 24 s after wave start, surviving attackers visibly retreat; they become untargetable after reaching the causeway's outer exit, not during their retreat through battle. At 29 s remaining retreaters exit behind a cliff and despawn. Hard cap skips excess spawns, never stacks invincible armies.
- Wave warning: three amber pulses from the causeway 3 s before arrival. Between raids defenders return to a short two-leg patrol around (23,11) and (25,14); tower does a slow scan. Workers flee to Court if enemy distance <4 and no Sentinel lies within 2 tiles of the worker-enemy segment; resume after 3 s clear. Raiders prioritize military targets; if none stand, attack nearest non-anchor building from its exterior apron. Keep the unfinished second Bastion and its workers behind this screen.
- Each attack: face/aim first; contact-aligned 1–3 px muzzle flash; luminous trajectory with dark outer pixels; small impact spark/shield ring; one-frame victim recoil; health decreases on actual impact. No screen shake, camera recoil, fullscreen flashes, or hit-number spam.
- Sentinel: 0.05 s ivory/gold muzzle flash, gold tracer travels 16 tiles/s, 0.1 s impact. Sunlance: three-pixel cyan lens charge grows through three discrete levels; 1-pixel cyan beam with intermittent ivory core lasts 0.1 s; endpoint burst lasts 0.15 s. Bastion fires a cyan bolt every 1.8 s, speed 12 tiles/s, damage 30; recoil 0.1 s, recharge segments 1.6 s.
- Jackal: orange muzzle fan 0.067 s and three pellet marks along one trajectory, speed 12 tiles/s; one damage event per burst. Strider: visibly arcing orange shell, 0.9 s flight with apex z=3 over interpolated terrain; 0.25 s stepped diamond blast and 0.6 s dust. Splash radius 1.2 tiles, damage 14, friendly fire off. Predicted impact position is fixed at launch, allowing movement to evade it.
- Maximum 48 live projectiles; exceeding cap delays that weapon by one tick, preserving visible cause/effect. Attacks use deterministic nearest-target selection, distance then stable-ID tie break. Buildings/units visibly recover by workers or replacements; bounded raids and protected anchors guarantee no ending.

## Camera, controls and screenshot framing

Rotation buttons apply one signed 90° snap per click; no interpolated rotation, motion blur, free orbit, or camera shake. ## Simulation/render handoff and implementation assumptions

- Rust owns state, AI, resource transactions, combat, construction and event timings. Fixed **60 Hz** (`sim_step(1000/60)`); use integer ticks for timers and integer/fixed-point positions internally. Seeded xorshift32 with nonzero seed, separate deterministic world/AI/effect streams; stable-ID iteration and tie breaking. Never `Math.random`. Camera, selection and render rate cannot consume simulation RNG or change AI decisions.
- State codes: **0 idle, 1 move, 2 attack, 3 work, 4 death, 5 construct, 6 retreat, 7 dock, 8 repair, 9 inactive**. `kind`: building/unit values above; **40 ore node, 41 service drone, 42 sky freighter, 43 relief capsule, 50 projectile, 51 impact, 52 wreck**. Terrain/grass/crystals/scaffolds/smoke/HUD are batched render instances derived from world seed, parent state and tick, not extra selectable gameplay units.
- ABI entity fields follow `INTERFACE.md` unchanged: x/y/z are world position (buildings use footprint center, units foot position); yaw radians; health 0..1; faction 0 Compact, 1 Reaver, 2 neutral; selected 0/1; anim_phase is current action time/period in [0,1]. Unselected props use selected=0. Buildings: param0=construction/refurbishment progress [0,1], param1=module cycle integer (0 original). Units: param0=cargo chunks for workers/carriers or action variant otherwise; param1=normalized cooldown (sling progress in dock state). Ore: param0=chunks remaining, param1=regrowth progress. Neutral flyers: param0=route identifier,param1=route progress. Projectiles/impacts/wrecks: param0=visual subtype or originating kind, param1=age seconds; x/y/z and yaw already reflect simulated trajectory. No persistent target indices cross the ABI.
- Additive export needed for clicking: `sim_select(index: i32)` where -1 clears; Rust translates current packed index to stable internal ID, retains it across array compaction, and emits selected flag on subsequent snapshots. Death clears selection. Frontend reports the selected entity's current packed index through `__APP.getState()`; selection is omitted from gameplay determinism comparisons when inputs differ. Do not change the existing required exports.
- Parent cosmetic phase is derived from fixed seed, kind and static building center; unit action phase comes from Rust. Projectiles, impact onset and death onset are simulated entities/events so low and high rendering frame rates see the same causal sequence. Renderer interpolation may smooth movement between ticks, but animation poses/colors switch discretely; canonical capture uses an exact tick boundary. Cosmetic event pools recycle deterministically, oldest first, and never alter gameplay.
- Rendering: raw WebGPU + WGSL; small instanced solid geometry/sprite batches, depth buffer, a single palette-quantization pass at **480×270**, nearest-neighbor presentation. No three.js, WebGL fallback, temporal AA, smooth bloom or per-frame allocation. Keep budgets ≤12 buildings, 40 units, 48 projectiles, 96 transient effect instances, 24 neutral moving props, 64 draw calls and 100k triangles. Target **>60 fps** at 960×540 presentation on the interface's hardware; the orchestrator measures it, this plan does not claim measured performance.
- Build geometry should read with flat face colors and 1-pixel edge clusters before adding particles. At any 10-second quiet interval show mining, hauling, assembly/refurbishment, patrol and ≥3 different environmental motions. At any 30-second interval show an arriving raid, readable outgoing/incoming attacks, one delivery, and a visible construction component change. Suppress only surplus cosmetic particles to hold bounds; never remove the active construction or all attack feedback.
